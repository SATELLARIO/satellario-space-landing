02What we process and why

Visiting the website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver pages and keep the service secure, GitHub, Inc. automatically records technical data about each request, including your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. We do not receive or access these logs. GitHub processes them under its own privacy statement, which also sets how long they are kept.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in publishing a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Emailing us

If you write to access@satellario.space, we receive your name, email address, any details in your signature and the content of your message and attachments. We use them only to reply and, if relevant, to discuss a possible collaboration or service.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or, where you contact us on your own behalf about a possible contract, steps taken at your request before entering into it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Retention: [NEEDS INPUT: retention period for enquiries], unless a longer period is required by law or to establish or defend legal claims.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or social-media plugins.
  • No cookies.
  • No content loaded from third-party servers: fonts and scripts are served from satellario.space.
  • No profiling or automated decision-making.
  • The "Live Feed" on the home page is a simulation generated in your browser; it sends nothing anywhere.

03Cookies and storage on your device

The site uses no cookies. It stores at most two small values in your browser's localStorage, and only after you click the corresponding button:

satellario.theme
Remembers the colour theme you chose (auto, light or dark). Set when you click the theme button.
satellario.lang
Remembers the language you chose (en or it). Set when you click the EN / IT button.
Duration
Until you clear this site's data in your browser. The values never leave your device.

These values are strictly necessary to provide a function you explicitly request, so no consent is needed (Art. 5(3) Directive 2002/58/EC; Art. 122 Italian Privacy Code; Italian Data Protection Authority cookie guidelines of 10 June 2021). You can remove them at any time by clearing the site data for satellario.space in your browser settings.
